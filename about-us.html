Who We Are

A small team. A very personal standard of care.

Alive Dreams Holidays is a Kolkata-based tours and travel company dedicated to designing meaningful journeys across India and the world. Though young as a brand, our leadership brings decades of hands-on travel expertise — from curating intimate family escapes and honeymoon getaways to orchestrating large corporate group movements.

We work as a small, attentive team, which means every traveller is personally looked after — not lost in a queue.

We serve leisure travellers, families, honeymooners, adventure seekers, and corporate clients — delivering the same high standard of care whether we are booking a weekend hill station retreat or orchestrating a pan-India MICE conference.

Our Mission

Our mission at ALIVE Dreams Holidays is simple: to bring your travel dreams to life. We do this by combining deep destination knowledge with a genuine passion for exploration — designing each trip around what matters most to you.

From the snow-capped Himalayas and the royal forts of Rajasthan to the backwaters of South India, the golden beaches of Southeast Asia, and the vibrant streets of Singapore, we handle every detail so you can focus on what travel is truly about — discovery, connection, and joy.

Our Vision

At ALIVE Dreams Holidays, we envision a world where every person has the opportunity to explore, experience, and be transformed by travel. We aspire to be the first name that comes to mind when someone in Eastern India — and beyond — dreams of a getaway.

Whether it's a solo adventure to the Himalayas, a family holiday in Southeast Asia, or a large-scale corporate incentive event, we see ourselves as the trusted architects of those experiences. Our vision is to continuously raise the bar for personalised travel — combining local expertise, global reach, and heartfelt service to create journeys that go far beyond a simple itinerary.

Core Values

The way we work

Six values that shape every itinerary, every quote, and every conversation.

Passion for Travel

We don't just plan trips — we live and breathe travel. Our team brings genuine enthusiasm to every itinerary, every destination, and every client interaction. That passion is what makes the difference between an ordinary holiday and an extraordinary one.

Customer First

Your satisfaction is the foundation of every decision we make. We listen carefully, plan meticulously, and stay available throughout your journey — because when you travel with confidence, you travel better.

Integrity & Transparency

We believe in honest, straightforward dealings — no hidden charges, no fine-print surprises. What we quote is what you pay. What we promise is what we deliver. Trust is the cornerstone of every relationship we build.

Personalisation

No two travellers are the same, and no two itineraries should be either. We take the time to understand your preferences, interests, and budget, crafting experiences that feel tailor-made — because they are.

Excellence in Every Detail

From the first enquiry to the final farewell, we strive for perfection in every aspect of your journey — accommodation, transfers, activities, and beyond. Small details make big memories.

Spirit of Discovery

We encourage every traveller to step beyond the familiar and embrace the extraordinary. Whether it is a hidden waterfall in Meghalaya, a centuries-old temple in Tamil Nadu, or a night market in Bangkok — we are here to help you find it.
